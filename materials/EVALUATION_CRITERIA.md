# Evaluation Criteria

Converted from `pdf/EVALUATION_CRITERIA.pdf` (QuantCo, 1 page).

The overall ranking combines two parts:

| Part | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Score (tournament result) | 70 % |
| Presentation and method | 30 % |

## 1. Score (70 %)

Teams are ranked by cumulative **net payoff** (money received as customer minus money paid as insurance) across all games, as shown
on the leaderboard.

- The top team receives **70 points**. All other teams are **scaled linearly by rank** (first place = 70, last place = 0).
- Line items that are not submitted count with the game defaults (a = 0, b = 0). No special treatment.

## 2. Presentation and method (30 %)

Shortly before the tournament ends, each team hands in a short write-up (**max. 1 page**) and pitches its approach. The jury awards
0 to 30 points based on:

| Criterion | Guiding question |
| --- | --- |
| Method and uncertainty | How did you estimate the threshold t and derive a and b from it? |
| Evidence for your choices | What makes you confident your approach works? How did you convince yourselves before relying on it? |
| Resourcefulness | How much did you get out of how little? **Smart, economical solutions beat brute force, so no token maxing.** |
| Reliability and automation | Does your setup run dependably and finish within the one minute? |
| Clarity and honesty | Is the approach explained clearly? Do the described strategy and the actual results match, with strengths and weaknesses stated openly? |

## 3. Fair play

Violations of the fair-play rules (see `GAME_DESCRIPTION.md`) lead to disqualification.
