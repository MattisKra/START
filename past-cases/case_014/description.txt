Accidental damage claim on the home contents cover. A bowl was knocked over and fell against the display cabinet, and the glass panel in the cabinet door cracked from the impact.

A replacement pane was made to measure and fitted back into the door frame, with a mounting hole drilled for the hardware.

The point to note on this one is the cover: this policy was taken out with the optional Glass Breakage Cover selected, so glass components of the furniture are within the insurance.
