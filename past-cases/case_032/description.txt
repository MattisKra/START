Buildings frost claim after a long deep freeze. Inside, a water fitting failed in the cold and was repaired, with a thermal camera used to trace the escape first. There was also a return visit to finish off.

Outside, the exterior render cracked and spalled off the facade under the freeze-thaw. The wall has been made good and repainted. A scaffold went up across that elevation only, both storeys and about eight metres wide, and it was struck before the painters went round to the rear, which they did off their own ladders. The plaster knocked off came away in a few rubble sacks.

Worth flagging on the billing: the painters have also gone over an undamaged rear elevation that had no frost loss, and their invoice carries an admin flat rate and a line for site catering. This policy has a facade frost endorsement on it.
