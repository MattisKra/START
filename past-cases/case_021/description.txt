Kitchen water damage, ground floor.

A leak developed in the supply pipe beneath the kitchen sink, causing water damage to the floor and the lower section of the adjacent wall. Leak detection was carried out to locate the source, after which the pipe was exposed, repaired, and re-insulated. A short section of copper piping was replaced. The detection firm charges its attendance and survey on site as a "call-out" fee and bills its van separately.

The affected floor and wall base were dried before the new flooring was installed. A wooden kitchen table that had warped beyond repair was removed and replaced with a comparable table. It was a plain pine table we bought about seven years ago, and the same model is still in the shop at EUR 440 before VAT. When we took out the policy we added the new-for-old option for our furnishings to the schedule, together with the bicycle cover.
