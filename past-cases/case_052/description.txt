The pool house one again. Indoor pool at the big villa, and a connection on the supply pipe running to the pool let go while the family were away.

It ran for a few hours before anyone got home and the water spread out of the pool room into the adjoining conservatory, and some of it worked its way down through floor openings into the plant room underneath where the pumps and filtration sit. The wall surfaces down there got wet and there was concern about the electrical gear stored in the room. Upstairs, the big wool rug in the conservatory seating corner was sitting in a puddle by the time they found it and went off to a specialist to be cleaned and dried; the cane sofa next to it was fine once it dried out.

On cover, the owner's schedule has the loose-furnishings extension ticked and none of the other optional extras - the broker apparently suggested adding the pool equipment option at the last renewal and he never got round to it.

Couple of things worth flagging for the file. When the conservatory flooring went back in (the room is about 4 by 3 metres) the owner didn't want the plain tiling that was there before and had it redone in a high-end natural stone instead, and the new skirting went in as a premium hardwood rather than the softwood that came out - both upgrades on the pre-loss standard. The electrician also swapped out a run of components in the plant room as a precaution, over and above the ones that had actually been in contact with the water.
