Electrical surge claim on the fixed building-services cover. Lightning struck close to the house during a storm, and the surge came through the mains and knocked out two of the fixed air conditioning units in the house, one in the living room and one in the kitchen. Both are fixed, permanently wired units that serve the building. The policy is the standard wording, with no endorsements on the schedule.

Neither could be sensibly repaired, so both were replaced like-for-like. The living-room unit sits on a floor-standing console, the same kind of stand it had before; the old stand was scorched and could not be reused. A technician shipped the replacement units in, fitted them and checked the wiring over.

Worth noting where each one is. The living-room unit stands at the far end of the open-plan room, about seven metres from the hob. The kitchen unit is mounted on the kitchen wall, a couple of metres from the hob.
