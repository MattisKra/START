Cellar leak at the policyholder's place, a small one. A compression fitting on a wall-mounted
pipe bend down in the basement had been leaking, and the water got into the wall and floor
around it. The patch that was soaked right through was about a square meter, but the tiled panel
around the bend, roughly 2.3 m wide and 1.2 m high, had to come off as one piece.

They found and sealed the joint, put about a meter of new lagging on the pipe and left one
service opening in the panel so the fitting can be reached again. The surface underneath was
levelled and the panel retiled with standard tiles. The owner had run his own little fan heater
down there over the weekend before the firm came, which is not on any invoice.

The drying firm's dryer went in on the morning of Monday 2 February and was collected on the
morning of Monday 9 February. The tiler was on site from half seven until half four on the
Tuesday and came back at half seven on the Wednesday and was done by about half twelve.

It all stayed in that one basement area, and nothing fancy went back in, just standard materials
throughout.

The invoices came in as a few summary lines rather than fully itemised. The tiler priced his
tiles, silicone and the laying by the square meter and meter in his two lump lines, and put his
own time on site on the last line together with his van.
