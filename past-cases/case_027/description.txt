Kitchen on the ground floor, the sink under the bay window at the back of the house.

At the start of February the laminate in front of the sink cabinet started to lift at the joints and the plaster at the bottom of the wall next to the sink went dark. There was nothing dripping that we could see, so a leak detection firm was called. Two technicians arrived at eight in the morning and were here until three in the afternoon without a proper break. They measured the floor and the wall base, listened along the pipes and in the end found a pinhole in the copper supply under the sink, behind the back panel of the cabinet.

The plumber came the next day. He took the cabinet back panel and a strip of flooring out to get at the pipe, stripped off the soaked black foam lagging and cut out the bad copper. The piece he took out, from the isolating valve down to the elbow at the floor, measured a shade over 1.5 m laid out on the worktop. New copper went in over the same run with the same fittings, and new lagging of the same black foam went back on (see photo).

The drying firm put one condensation dryer in front of the sink. It was switched on in the afternoon of 4 February and collected on the morning of 11 February, once their last readings on the floor and wall base were back to normal. The loose boards on the worktop are for the flooring fitter, who has not been yet.

The oak table and chairs by the patio door stood well away from the sink and were only used by the trades as a desk for their paperwork and tools. The garden furniture outside on the patio was not involved.
