Accidental damage claim on the portable electronics cover. During the Berlin to Munich drive the laptop was thrown against the car door when the driver swerved to avoid a tree that had come down in the storm the night before. The screen cracked across the middle and the lower right corner was damaged on the impact.

The machine is a 14-inch aluminium notebook, bought new last spring with a EUR 1,500 work bonus that it used up apart from about a hundred euros. The same model is still on sale. It was in a thin neoprene sleeve on the back seat at the time.

The shop ran a diagnostic first to confirm the damage. It then fitted a complete new display assembly rather than trying to replace the glass alone, and swapped the dented lower housing shell for a new one. The battery and everything else checked out fine. The owner grumbled that the two repairs together came to nearly what he had paid for the machine, most of it for the display, but the shop pointed out that it was still less than a new one.

Two points for the file. The owner paid the shop and is claiming the money back from us, so the settlement goes to the policyholder rather than to the shop. The invoice also carries a light polish of an old surface scuff on the lid that was there before and does not affect how the machine works.
