Bathroom leak, upstairs at Jonas's place, cold water line.

The cold water supply pipe in the 1st-floor bathroom failed and started leaking into the wall. They had to open the wall up to get at it, then repair the pipe and close everything back up.

It wasn't a huge flood — nothing beyond the immediate wall area was affected. Once the pipe was fixed, the opening was closed and repainted, and a dryer was brought in for a bit to make sure everything was properly dry before that.

Took a couple of site visits to sort out fully.
