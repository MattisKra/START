A supply coupling let go all at once behind the kitchen wall, and the plumber was clear it was one clean burst, not the pipes quietly giving out over the years. Water pushed straight out across the open floor.

Because it's all one big open space, pretty much the whole ground-floor linoleum had to come up, skirting and all, and go in the container. Leak detection with a technician on site first to pin down the coupling, then floor removal and a long stretch of drying — large-area plus the enclosed under-floor cavities. The kitchen sits in the same open area and got wet too, so it was disinfected as part of the job. Dryers ran a good while.
