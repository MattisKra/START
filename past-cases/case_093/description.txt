The big multi-floor water job at the policyholder's townhouse. The family were away when the upstairs guest toilet cistern fill valve let go and ran unattended for hours.

Water came down from the guest WC through the floor build-up into the stairwell and down through the ceiling into the living room and the hobby room. A robot vacuum in the living room set off on its own and dragged the water across a wider patch of parquet than the drip would have, wetting a rug and some skirting too, and the machine was a write-off. The skirting along its track came up with the parquet and broke, so more new skirting went in than had been taken off beforehand. The hobby room ceiling took moisture and the billiard table got soaked, cloth, cues, rail cushions and the light over it.

This one had a lot of trades on site and the paperwork got busy. There was only ever the one skip on the drive, shared by everyone for the whole job, but a couple of the crews each billed for skip hire and debris disposal, the drying firm metered its own power and put a second visit on the bill, and there are a few extras floating about - a processing fee, catering for the crew, and, while everyone was in, the decorators also freshened up the master bedroom next door even though the water never reached it. Flagging all of that for the file.
