Burglary at the policyholder's home. Someone forced the front door of the insured house overnight and got in, and a fair bit was taken before they left - mainly jewellery and some clothing out of the bedroom.

The jewellery was the bulk of it. From the list the owner handed in afterwards:
- the engagement ring, a single diamond on a platinum band - their jeweller valued it at around EUR 8,900 last year
- an 18-carat gold bracelet, bought in 2017 for EUR 520; the same jeweller says a like-for-like one costs about EUR 740 today
- a pair of cultured pearl stud earrings on gold posts
- a sterling silver pendant on a chain, a gift
- a steel quartz wristwatch on a leather strap from a mid-range brand, worn daily

None of these were ever listed separately on the contract. The schedule shows EUR 1,000 per article and EUR 5,000 per event for unspecified valuables, and no deductible.

The clothing taken was ordinary everyday wear: two pairs of jeans, three shirts, a wool jumper, a winter jacket and a pair of trainers, none of it older than a couple of seasons.

A locksmith came out the same night to board up and re-secure the forced door.
