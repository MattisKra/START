Bathroom leak upstairs — cold water supply pipe in the 1st-floor bathroom failed and leaked into
the wall.
Straightforward fix: opened the wall, replaced the failed pipe section like-for-like with sealing and
standard insulation, ran a blower to dry it out, then closed and repainted the opening.
Localised damage, nothing beyond the immediate wall patch. One firm did the repair and drying,
the painter closed up.
A couple of visits, all standard materials. Invoices came in as a few summary lines rather than
fully itemised.
