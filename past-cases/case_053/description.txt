Water damage from washing machine leak.

While the occupants were away, a leak developed at the washing machine or one of its connected hoses in the utility room. Because it went unnoticed for some time, water spread across the utility room floor and into the adjoining hallway, where it also affected a painting hanging on the wall.

The affected floor areas were cleaned, the washing machine connection was inspected to confirm the leak had been resolved, and water-damaged skirting boards were removed and replaced.

The painting required specialist treatment due to its significant value. It was professionally assessed, dried and stabilised, and then restored by a specialist.
