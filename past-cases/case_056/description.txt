The failed copper heating pipe in the basement plant room needed two plumber visits: an emergency call-out to stop the leak and make it safe, then a return visit a couple of days later to finish the reconnection and re-insulate. A separate tiling contractor then lifted the floor tiles that had been sitting under standing water right around the leak, laid fresh tiles, and  while on site also cleaned, disinfected and re-coated the surrounding tiles that were never wet.
