Sabine found the bathroom floor soaked at the end of July — the supply pipe under the built-in bathtub had been leaking for several days. About 6 m2 of bathroom tiling was wet through and needs full replacement.
Water spread through the shared partition into the living room. The laminate there (about 18 m2)
was swollen and bubbling and had to be removed and relaid, and the skirting along that run was replaced. The lower section of the living-room wall along the partition, roughly up to about hip height, was stained and needed repainting after surface preparation. When the drying company inspected, the moisture meter showed clear readings across a patch of the ceiling directly above the affected wall, and there was visible brown staining spreading from the corner where the partition meets the ceiling. Photos and the moisture-measurement log document the ceiling damage. The ceiling had to be repainted as well.
