The pool house claim at the big villa. A connection on the indoor pool's supply pipe separated while the owners were away and ran for hours, and the water spread from the pool room into the conservatory and down into the plant room below.

The usual consequential mess followed - a soaked rug in the conservatory seating area, wet wall surfaces in the plant room, and worry about the electrical components stored down there. The pool's own supply pipework needed repairing at the separated connection. The filtration had kept running throughout and had drawn in the dirty water standing on the plant-room floor, so the filter had to be flushed through and put back into service before the pool could run again.

The point to note on this one is the cover. Checking the file, the schedule carries two optional extras and nothing else: the loose furnishings of the insured rooms, and the indoor pool together with its technical plant. So the pool technology side of the work is not sitting outside cover the way it would on a bare Water Damage section. The owner did still choose to upgrade the floor to a high-end natural stone while the work was open. Nothing on file, neither the photos nor any report, shows what floor was down before the stone went in.
