The failed copper heating pipe in the basement plant room was cut out and reconnected with new fittings and re-insulated with rock wool — a straightforward repair. While the plumber was on site, though, client had him take care of a few other jobs around the house: repainting, ripping out and replacing the laminate floor in the adjacent storage room (which was never touched by the leak), and servicing the outdoor garden tap. The escaping water only ever affected the boiler/plant room itself; everything else on the list was general home maintenance the plumber folded onto the same invoice.
