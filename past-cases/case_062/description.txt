Kitchen water damage, ground floor.

A leak developed in the water supply pipe beneath the kitchen sink, causing moisture damage to the surrounding floor and the lower section of the adjacent wall. A leak-detection service was used to identify the exact source before the pipework was exposed, repaired, and re-insulated. Approximately one to two meters of copper piping were replaced.

The affected floor area and wall base were dried for several days before the flooring was reinstated.

A wooden kitchen table was also damaged by the water and warped beyond repair. It was a plain, untreated pine table that we bought new from a self-assembly furniture store in early 2019 and ate at every day since, so it had the usual dents, scratches and cup rings. It was subsequently replaced with a higher-quality solid-oak designer table, which represented an upgrade compared with the original standard table.
