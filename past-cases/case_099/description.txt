Escape-of-water claim down in the basement. A fixed heating pipe in the plant room burst without warning and flooded the room, leaving a good depth of water standing before it was shut off.

One heating firm handled the whole job in a single visit. They pumped the water out, then renewed the water-damaged boiler along with its flue and storage tank, adjusted the supply pipework to suit the replacement, and took the old unit away for disposal.

It was the one contractor on site, billed as a handful of summary lines.
