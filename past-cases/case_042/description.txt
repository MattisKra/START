Electrical surge claim on the fixed building-services cover. A lightning strike during a thunderstorm sent a surge through the house and damaged the fixed living-room air conditioning unit, a permanently wired 3.5 kW wall-mounted split unit that serves the building and sits well clear of the kitchen, some nine metres from the hob. It could not be economically repaired and was replaced.

For the file, the owner did not simply put back what was there. The replacement is a larger, higher-output 7.1 kW model, a clear step up in cooling capacity from the old one. The old wall bracket was swapped for a new decorative floor console chosen for the look of the room rather than because the new unit needed it.

The supplier also billed an express, timed delivery to get the unit in quickly, and the contractor added an administration fee for handling the paperwork.
