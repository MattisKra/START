Update on the open-plan ground-floor claim, it was built in 1994.
Cause this time is a single sudden burst on the supply line, confirmed by the plumber, one
failure, not gradual ageing. Water ran out across the main open living area and the floor there
was lifted and dried.
One thing to flag: the kitchen is tucked off to the far side behind a wall and stayed completely
dry, no moisture readings there at all, so the kitchen disinfection line really shouldn't be on this
claim. Both the floor-removal firm and the drying firm have also billed a construction-waste
disposal for the same job, and the drying invoice has a second van charge for a return visit on
top of its first.
Assessment and leak detection up front as usual. Note the deductible on this policy is the higher
band.
