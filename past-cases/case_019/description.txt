During a thunderstorm a power surge came through the mains and hit the home entertainment setup. The speaker system and the TV that were plugged in and running at the time both took surge damage, and a technician came out afterwards to check things over and confirm the wiring was safe.

A few things to flag for the file. Nothing on that circuit was on a surge-protected strip, it was all plugged straight into the wall. The replacement TV the owner has gone for is a bigger, higher-end model than the one that failed. The set on the wall was an ordinary 55-inch LED TV from one of the mainstream brands, about four years old, nothing special about it.

The claim also lists the HDMI cables, the wall bracket and the remote, plus a games console. The console was switched off and unplugged at the time, sitting in a cupboard across the room, so it was not part of what the surge hit.
