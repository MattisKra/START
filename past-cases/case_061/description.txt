Client came home end of July, walked into the bathroom and the floor was soaked. Turned out the pipe feeding the built-in bathtub had been leaking for a while, maybe several days going by how far it had spread.

The bathroom's small, maybe 6 square meters of tiled floor, all of it wet. Tiles wouldn't come up clean so the whole floor needs redoing.

Water also travelled through the shared wall into the living room next door. The laminate in there — about 18 m², so a good part of the room — was swollen and bubbling. The lower section of the wall along that same partition, roughly 20 m² up to about hip height, had visible staining. Skirting along that run (about 25 running meters) will need replacing too.

Ceiling in the living room looked completely fine when it was checked, no staining anywhere. Same with the hallway next door, nothing there either.

Plumber fixed the pipe straight away, then dehumidifiers ran in the bathroom and living room for about 5 days before anyone could start on floors and paint.
