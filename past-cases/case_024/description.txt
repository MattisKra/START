A bicycle was stolen from outside the building. A hidden AirTag let the police track it down, but the lock had seized and couldn't be opened at the scene, so a locksmith was called out to cut it off before the bike could be recovered and returned.

The bike had taken a knock during the theft and needed the frame and the brakes replaced. Worth noting for cover: checking the file, this particular bicycle is specifically scheduled under a separate Bicycle Endorsement on the policy at an agreed value of EUR 3,500 (the endorsement schedule also lists the frame number and the original purchase date, two summers ago).
