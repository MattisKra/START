Theft claim from a trip abroad. While the policyholder was on vacation in France, the parked private car was broken into and a suitcase was taken from inside it. The suitcase had clothes and some jewellery in it.

The break-in was reported to the local police afterwards and there is a report reference. The claim came in through a settlement agent, covering the stolen clothing and jewellery along with some vehicle-related costs from the trip.

Nothing was taken from the policyholder's home, which was locked up and untouched the whole time.
