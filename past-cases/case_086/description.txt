Cellar leak at the policyholder's place, a smaller one. A wall-mounted pipe bend down in the
basement had been letting water out around a compression fitting, and it got into the wall and
floor right around it.
When the plumber got to it, though, the fitting turned out to have been put on badly in the first
place. It looks like a DIY job from a while back that was never done up properly, so it had been
quietly weeping for a long time rather than failing all of a sudden. Basically a bad connection
that was loose from the start.
Either way, they sealed it up, patched the wall tiling back around the spot and re-levelled the
surface underneath before retiling, and then a dryer sat running in the basement for a couple of
weeks.
Just flagging the cause for the coverage call, since this wasn't a sudden accidental thing.
