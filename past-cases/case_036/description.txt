Water damage from washing machine leak.

While the policyholder was away, the washing machine in the utility room developed a leak that went unnoticed for some time. Water spread across the utility room and into the adjoining hallway, affecting the floor covering and a valuable painting hanging there.

The affected floors in both rooms were cleaned, the washing machine connection was checked, and damaged skirting boards were removed and replaced. Leak detection also carried out moisture measurements and drying of the affected floor areas.

Several invoice items appear to fall outside the documented damage area, including cleaning and repainting of an upstairs bedroom, drying work in a basement storage room, restoration of a second painting stored in the basement, and a separate administrative handling fee.

The painting in the hallway was professionally assessed, dried, stabilised, and restored by a specialist. The household is a private one and nothing is listed individually on the schedule.
