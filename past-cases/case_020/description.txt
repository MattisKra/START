Short version of the ground-floor water job open-plan, 1994 build, original copper.

Cause came back as material ageing: the pipes had gone porous in several places over the years, not a single burst, the leak-detection specialist was clear on that.

The work was the usual run: a damage assessment call, indoor leak detection with a technician and a van, lifting the whole ground-floor linoleum and prepping the screed, then a long spell of drying across the open floor and cavities. Kitchen's in the same open area and got disinfected too.
