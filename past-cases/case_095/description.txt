Escape-of-water claim in the basement plant room, and a bigger job with several firms involved. A fixed heating pipe burst suddenly and flooded the room, close to a meter deep before the water was shut off.

The heating firm renewed the submerged boiler with its flue and storage tank, adjusted the supply pipework and disposed of the old unit, and added a coordination and administration fee on top of the itemised work. The electrician pumped the water out with a submersible pump bought for the job, checked and rewired the circuits that had been under water, came back a second time for a follow-up look, and while on site also cleaned and repainted the stairwell walls further up, which had stayed dry.

Both trades attended the same day. The pipe went on a Monday evening, and with the heating and hot water out the tenants checked into a hotel in town that same night. They shared one room at EUR 138 a night, EUR 19 of which was breakfast for the two of them, and settled their own bar bill at checkout, so only the room-and-breakfast charges are on the hotel invoice.

The new boiler was running and the flat was warm again by Friday lunchtime. The tenants had already booked through the weekend and decided to stay on, checking out on the Sunday morning. Per the schedule, accommodation is covered at up to EUR 150 a day for at most 14 days.
