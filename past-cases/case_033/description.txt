Accidental damage claim on the portable electronics cover. During the swerve on the Berlin to Munich drive, the laptop and a tablet that were in the same bag were both thrown against the car door. The laptop screen and board took a heavy hit, and the tablet screen cracked.

The shop's written report says the laptop is not economical to repair, so it has been replaced. The owner chose a higher-spec model than the one that was damaged, and picked up a new carry bag and an extended warranty at the same time. A diagnostic was run on both devices.

Separately, a data recovery house pulled the files off the old laptop drive and charged an administration fee. For the file: this policy carries a battery health waiver, which matters because the old laptop's battery was below 80 percent.
