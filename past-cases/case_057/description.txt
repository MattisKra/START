An expensive watch and some cash were taken in a robbery while the policyholder was travelling abroad. They were threatened with a weapon at the time. The local police were notified and the report was translated so it could be used for the claim.

The watch was bought eight years ago for about EUR 6,000, and the cash came to a little over EUR 1,000. Nothing was listed separately on the contract. The schedule shows a valuables limit of EUR 2,500 per item and EUR 5,000 per event, EUR 500 for cash per event, EUR 10,000 for belongings carried away from home, and no deductible.

The loss and the claim handling were written up together on a single settlement invoice, with the compensation for the stolen items on one line and the translation and handling on another.
