A section of copper heating pipe in the basement boiler room had failed at a joint and was dripping onto the floor. When the plumber came out to fix it, Torsten asked him to "do it properly this time" and upgrade the repaired section rather than just restore it. So instead of plain copper the plumber fitted premium chrome-plated designer piping and matching chrome fittings, wrapped the run in high-performance aluminium-clad Armaflex insulation instead of the old rock wool, and while he was at it added a new smart leak-detection sensor and a thermostatic mixing valve that had never been part of the original installation. The actual leak was small - a single failed joint - but the finished job is a clear step up from what was there before the loss.
