Accidental damage claim on the portable electronics cover, which is on the schedule in its standard form with no extras. On a drive from Berlin to Munich the laptop was in use on the passenger seat. The car had to swerve hard to miss a tree, and the laptop was flung against the door. The screen cracked across the middle and the lower right corner took a knock.

The shop ran a diagnostic before quoting the repair. The impact damage is genuine and physical, a cracked panel and a dented corner.

One thing the diagnostic threw up: the battery is not what it used to be, though the laptop still got through a normal day on it. The shop's readout is in the photo.
