Leaking bathtub supply pipe, discovered end of July after a few days of seeping. The whole bathroom floor (about 6 m² of tiling) was wet and had to be taken out and relaid.

Water reached the living room through the shared wall: about 18 m² of laminate had to be removed and reinstalled together with the skirting, and the lower part of the living-room wall along the partition had to be prepared and repainted.

Ceiling and hallway were both checked and were dry. Pipe repaired promptly; drying ran a few days before the trades started.
