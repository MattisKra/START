Storm claim. A large tree in the garden was brought down by the storm and dropped onto the declared garden shed, wrecking the roof. Standard falling-tree damage to the covered garden building.

The fallen tree was cut up and cleared by a separate crew, and the ground around the shed got churned up in the process - roots, loose soil and some shifted paving. The root ball was a single modest lump, under a cubic metre. No foundation work was needed; the ground was just disturbed by the tree and the clearing.

The disposal service kept the paperwork simple and billed the haul-away as just two lines: one for the storm debris off the shed and the tree, and one for the ground material from the churned-up area.
