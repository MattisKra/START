Cellar leak at the policyholder's place, a smaller one. A compression fitting on a wall-mounted
pipe bend down in the basement gave way and let water out, and it got into the wall and floor
around it. The wet tiled patch on the wall ran from the corner about 2.40 m along and up to
roughly 1.16 m, so that whole panel had to come off.
Fixing it was straightforward enough. They sealed the fitting, stripped the affected tiling,
re-levelled the surface underneath and retiled, and then left a dryer running in the basement for
a couple of weeks to dry everything out. The new panel got a silicone joint along the bottom
where it meets the floor and one up the corner end; the open end just butts against the old
tiling with normal grout.
One thing is worth noting, though. For the retiling the owner went for big designer wall tiles and
a premium grout and sealant, which is quite a step up from the plain basement tiles that were on
there before. Their choice, of course, but it's a fair bit fancier than what came off.
A small job otherwise, all in the one basement spot.
