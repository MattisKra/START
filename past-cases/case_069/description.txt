A robbery abroad, and a fair bit was taken. The policyholder was threatened with a weapon in a side street and the robbers made off with an expensive watch (a fifth-anniversary present four years ago; the owner puts it well above EUR 10,000) and a diamond ring off the person, plus roughly EUR 1,200 in cash and their mobile phone, a model bought for about EUR 700 some two years ago. It was all reported to the local police. The gold necklace that is also on the claim was not part of the robbery: the owner only noticed it was gone when packing at the hotel two days later and cannot say where it went.

For the ring there is the jeweller's receipt from spring 2023, EUR 3,850 for a solitaire in platinum, and the owner says a comparable stone would cost more today.

Nothing on the contract was specially listed. The schedule shows a valuables limit of EUR 2,500 per item and EUR 5,000 in total per event, EUR 500 for cash per event and EUR 10,000 for belongings carried away from home, and no deductible.

There was a lot of paperwork behind the claim. The police report had to be translated and notarised, there are shipping and processing charges on the invoices, and the owner is also claiming for the extra hotel nights and travel they had to arrange after the incident. Flagging all of it for the file.
