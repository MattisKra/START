Escape of water claim. A copper supply pipe split inside the bathroom wall next to the washbasin. Water ran down inside the wall and soaked the plaster and the tiles below the leak, down to the floor.

A plumber opened the wall at the leak, replaced the split section of pipe, and closed and re-tiled the opened patch. The rest of the bathroom was not damaged.

While on site, the plumber also serviced and descaled the gas boiler in the hallway, which had nothing to do with the leak.
