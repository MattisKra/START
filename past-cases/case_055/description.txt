Bathroom leak upstairs - the cold water supply pipe in the 1st-floor bathroom failed and leaked into the wall.

Standard job to fix: opened the wall to get at the pipe, repaired it, dried the spot out with a blower and closed the opening back up. Nothing beyond that little patch of wall was actually wet - it was a localised thing.

Rather than just touching in the patched area, the painter went ahead and repainted the whole wall the opening was on, then carried on and did the other bathroom walls and the ceiling too. The wall had been decorated recently and the same paint was still available.
