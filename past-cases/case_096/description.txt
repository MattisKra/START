The supply pipe beneath the built-in bathtub leaked for several days before client found the bathroom floor soaked at the end of July. A specialist located and pinpointed the pipe defect under the bathtub (an afternoon on site) and ran a moisture check to map the spread. After the the pipe was fixed, condensation dryers ran in the bathroom and living room for a few days. 

About 18 m² of swollen laminate in the living room was removed and relaid with impact-sound insulation, and skirting was replaced. The lower living-room wall along the partition was prepared and repainted. The living-room ceiling and the hallway were dry at inspection.
