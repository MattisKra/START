The multi-floor water escape at the policyholder's place. The household was out at a family event when the upstairs guest toilet cistern's fill valve failed and ran unnoticed for hours.

It worked its way down from the guest WC through the floor structure into the stairwell and on through the ceiling into the living room and the hobby room below. A robot vacuum in the living room activated and drove about, spreading the water further across the parquet and wetting a rug and some skirting, and it didn't survive itself. The hobby room ceiling took moisture and the billiard table down there got wet, cloth, cues and overhead light and all.

The remediation was handled largely by one restoration contractor covering the strip-out, drying, floor and ceiling reinstatement and the plumbing fix, with a separate contents invoice for the billiard table, the rug and the vacuum.
