An expensive watch was taken in a robbery abroad. The policyholder was threatened with a weapon and the robbers took the watch off their wrist along with the cash they were carrying - roughly €900 by their own count. The watch was bought new about five years ago for around €21,000, and the same model costs more than that today. The incident was reported to the local police and the report was translated for the claim file.

The thing to note on this one is the cover. Checking the policy, this watch is specifically declared on a Valuables Schedule with a current valuation certificate, at a value well above the standard per-item jewellery limit.
