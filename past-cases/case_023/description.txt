Overnight someone forced the back door of the house, the one that opens onto the alley where the bins stand. They levered it open at the lock, and the frame around the strike plate was splintered. Nothing else in the house seemed to be touched, but my road bike was gone. I keep it in the back hallway just inside that door, locked through the wheel with a yellow U-lock.

I had hidden an AirTag under the saddle, so I could see it a few streets away the next morning. I went there together with the police, and the bike was leaning against a post in an alley. The U-lock had been cut through and was lying on the ground next to it. The frame was bent near the front and the brakes were damaged, so a bike workshop picked it up and took it away for repair.

Back home the door would no longer lock at all, and I could not leave the house open like that. A locksmith came out the same day, fitted a new lock to the back door and made it secure again. The van was parked right outside the door for most of the afternoon.
