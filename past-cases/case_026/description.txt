A bicycle was stolen from outside the building. It was tracked down with a hidden AirTag and the police managed to locate it, but the lock on it had seized and couldn't be opened on site, so a locksmith was called out to cut it off and the bike was recovered and brought back.

The bike itself came back in one piece, with no real damage from the theft. The owner took the opportunity while it was in the shop to upgrade the frame to a premium carbon one and have the whole thing serviced, new brakes, a drivetrain tune and all. Flagging for the file that the frame swap and the servicing were the owner's own choice, not damage caused by the theft.
