Utility room water damage.

While the policyholder was away, a washing machine hose developed a leak and water escaped for some time before being noticed. The water spread across the utility room floor and into the adjoining hallway.

The affected floor area was cleaned and dried, the washing machine connection was checked, and the damaged skirting boards were replaced. The work was recorded in a small number of summarized trade items.

A framed print hanging low on the hallway wall was also affected by moisture. It is one of a set of three identical poster prints that came in plain pine frames from a furniture store a few years ago; the other two hang in the bedroom and were not touched. We have never reported any single item of our belongings to the insurer separately, and our schedule only shows the building. Because the backing board had soaked up water, we asked a picture restorer nearby to have a look at it and dry it out.
