Storm claim, same event: a large garden tree blew over onto the declared garden shed and stove in the roof. The shed itself is the covered garden building.

The thing that stood out on this one was the root ball. When the clearing crew levered the tree out, the root mass came up enormous - well over a cubic metre - with a great deal of soil still packed into and around it. Rather than try to separate it, they lifted the whole tangle of roots, soil and green waste and carted it off together as one mixed clearance load.

The recycler's invoice therefore shows that combined load as a single line, alongside separate lines for the shed roof timber and broken glass, and for the displaced paving grit and loose topsoil. No foundation was dug out or repaired - the ground was only disturbed by the uprooting and the clear-up.
