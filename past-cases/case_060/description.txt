Electrical surge claim on the fixed building-services cover. Lightning hit the supply line in a storm, and the surge ran through the property and damaged two fixed air conditioning units, one on the kitchen wall near the hob and one in the hallway, along with a portable, remote-controlled split unit in the study. None could be economically repaired.

The replacements are like-for-like: a 7.1 kW unit again for the open kitchen and a 3.5 kW unit again for the hallway, both permanently wired and fixed like the old ones. The study unit is the type that ships with a handheld wireless remote and can be unplugged and moved. The two wall units came in together on one heavy pallet, over 80 kilograms, on standard freight; the study unit travelled separately in the fitter's van. The owner also paid an express surcharge to bring one item forward.

For the file: this policy carries a kitchen climate endorsement. The contractor's invoice also includes a return visit, an administration fee, and disposal of an old unrelated boiler that was cleared out at the same time.
