Client found water on the floor of the boiler room. A section of copper heating pipe down there had gone bad and was dripping — not a full burst, just a joint that had failed.

The plumber cut out about a meter of the old pipe and reconnected it with new fittings, then re-insulated the repaired section with rock wool once everything was back together.

Took a while — the main technician was on site something like a day and a half in total, with a helper along for part of that. Afterwards they cleaned and disinfected the tiled area right around where the leak had been dripping, since standing water in a cellar for a few days isn't exactly hygienic.
