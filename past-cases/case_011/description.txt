Accidental damage claim on the home contents cover. A bowl was knocked off a shelf and fell, catching the display cabinet on the way down. The glass panel in the cabinet door cracked, and a solid-wood side table next to the cabinet was knocked and dented in the same fall.

A glazier remade the cabinet pane to measure and refitted it, and cleared away the broken glass. A separate contractor repaired the dented side table.

A couple of things on the billing to flag. The contractor also touched up some older scratches on a second cabinet that were already there before the incident, and the invoice carries a return visit as well as an administrative flat rate.
