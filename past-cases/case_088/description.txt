The multi-floor water escape at the policyholder's townhouse. The family were away at a celebration when the fill valve in the upstairs guest toilet cistern gave out and ran unnoticed for hours.

The water tracked down from the guest WC across its floor and the skirting around it, through the floor structure into the stairwell, and on through the ceiling into the living room and the hobby room below. A robot vacuum sitting in the living room switched itself on and drove about, pushing the water across a good stretch more of the parquet than the drip alone would have reached, and a rug and some skirting got soaked as a result. The skirting along its track came up with the parquet and broke, so more new skirting went in than had been taken off beforehand. The vacuum was a write-off. Downstairs the hobby room ceiling took moisture damage and the billiard table got wet, cloth, cues and the overhead light included.

While the repairs were being organised the owner decided to take the opportunity to upgrade a few things rather than put everything back like for like. The replacement parquet is a premium solid oak rather than the standard-grade oak herringbone blocks that were there before, the new skirting is solid oak to match, the overhead billiard light was swapped for a higher-end smart fixture, and the cloth and cues were replaced with tournament-grade versions. Worth flagging for the file that those are upgrades on the pre-loss standard.
