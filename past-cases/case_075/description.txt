Another multi-floor water one, at the policyholder's townhouse. This time someone was home. The fill valve in the upstairs guest toilet cistern broke and started running, and it went on for a good while before anyone dealt with it.

The water came down the usual way, across the guest WC floor and the skirting first, then through the floor structure into the stairwell and down through the ceiling into the living room and the hobby room below.

Here is the wrinkle on this one. A robot vacuum was sitting in the living room and it started up and drove about while the water was coming in. The homeowner was in the house and saw it happening, the machine trailing water across the parquet, but left it running and didn't move it or switch it off. So it spread the water over a fair bit more of the parquet than the leak by itself would have reached, and soaked a rug and some extra skirting on the way. Those few lengths of skirting along its track snapped when that extra stretch of parquet was lifted, and the fitters put new ones in as part of relaying that same patch. The vacuum itself packed up.

Downstairs in the hobby room the ceiling took moisture damage and the billiard table got wet as well, cloth, cues and the light above it.
