The pool house one, written up short. Indoor pool at the villa, a supply-pipe connection let go while the family were out, and the water spread from the pool room into the conservatory and down into the plant room below.

One restoration contractor handled most of it - the leak detection and moisture survey, the pool pipe repair, the vacuuming and drying across all three rooms, and the floor, wall and skirting reinstatement - with a separate building-services invoice for the rug, an electrical and pool-technology inspection, and the new floor.

Worth noting the owner had the floor put back in a high-end natural stone rather than the plain tiling that was there before. He only made that call after the restoration firm had already quoted the floor like for like in plain tiles, and they never changed their price; the stone came through as an extra on the building-services bill.

For the file: the schedule carries the loose-furnishings extension and no other optional extras.
