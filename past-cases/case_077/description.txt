Water damage from washing machine leak.

While the policyholder and family were away, a leak developed at the washing machine or one of its hoses in the utility room and went unnoticed for some time. Water spread across the utility room floor and into the adjoining hallway, affecting the floor covering and a painting hanging on the wall.

The affected floor area was cleaned, the washing machine connection was checked to confirm that the leak had stopped, and the damaged skirting boards were removed and replaced.

The painting was a valuable piece and required specialist treatment. It was assessed by a professional restorer, dried and stabilised, and then fully restored.
