Accidental damage claim on the home contents cover. A heavy bowl was knocked off a shelf and fell against a display cabinet. The glass panel in the cabinet door cracked on the impact.

The same knock did more than crack the glass. It caught the solid-wood door frame on the way, denting and gouging the timber, and a wooden shelf inside took a hit and was scratched and chipped.

So there are really two kinds of damage on the one cabinet: the broken glass pane, which had to be remade to measure and refitted, and the timber parts of the cabinet, the frame and the shelf, which needed repair and refinishing.
